# Prior authorization

This glossary defines the request and review terms used in the prior authorization workflow.

## Language

**Prior authorization request**:
The patient, provider, diagnosis, procedure, clinical notes, and insurance information submitted for review.
_Avoid_: Upload

**Review result**:
The findings and draft recommendation produced by reviewing a prior authorization request. A clinician uses this result to make a decision about that request.
_Avoid_: Final authorization decision

**Review summary**:
The concise narrative within a review result that summarizes the automated review's findings and draft recommendation. It is available before a clinician records a decision.
_Avoid_: Final decision summary
